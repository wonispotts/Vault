# Woni Spotts: Journal of Cross-Cultural Research

* **ISSN (Online):** [3143-7443](https://portal.issn.org/resource/ISSN/3143-7443)
* **Established:** 2026
* **Frequency:** Published quarterly
* **Timeline:** 4 weeks average from submission to publication

### Aims & Scope
Historical preservation and correction of Black American history and the anthropological documentation of cultures around the world.
Keywords: Black American History, African American History, Woni Spotts, Ethnographic, Anthropology

### Peer Review Policy
Woni Spotts: Journal of Cross-Cultural Research utilizes double-anonymous peer review, keeping author and reviewer identities hidden. Every manuscript is evaluated by at least two independent external experts unaffiliated with the publisher, selected for their active expertise in Black American History, African American History, Ethnographic research, and Anthropology. Final acceptance or rejection decisions are based strictly on the evaluation and consensus of these external reviewers.

### Plagiarism Screening
The journal maintains a strict, zero-tolerance plagiarism-free policy. All original work is routinely screened for plagiarism prior to the peer review process.
